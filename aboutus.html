Our Story

Get to Know Us.

Founded in 1994, HALCYON HOME began as a small workshop dedicated to the belief that high-quality design shouldn't be a luxury. We believe in the power of a well-curated living space to transform daily life.

ESTABLISHED 1994 • STOCKHOLM ORIGINS

The Vision

Our journey started with a single chair and a commitment to democratic design. Today, we curate collections that balance aesthetic purity with the messy, beautiful reality of everyday life.

Our
Commitment

Sustainability

By 2030, we aim for 100% circularity. All our wood is FSC-certified, and our textiles are sourced from responsible partners who prioritize the planet.

Quality Materials

We don't believe in "fast furniture." Every piece is tested for longevity, using solid oaks, tempered glass, and reinforced steel that matures with age.

Affordable Design

By optimizing our supply chain and focusing on flat-pack innovation, we pass the savings directly to you without compromising on the designer aesthetic.
